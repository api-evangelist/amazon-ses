A misconfigured sender can spike bounces that damage the sending reputation of your entire Amazon SES account. With Amazon SES tenant management and Mail Manager, you can isolate reputation per tenant. Compare five architectural patterns for applying the X-SES-TENANT header, from static endpoints to AWS Lambda-driven runtime resolution.
